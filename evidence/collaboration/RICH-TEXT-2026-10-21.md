## Boundaries

PNG is the positive browser fixture; JPEG/WebP are accepted formats but not separate positive codec fixtures here. File signatures/size are server-checked, not a claim of antivirus or full server image transcoding. Real OS clipboard/drag hardware, mobile browsers, crop/video/table/formula editing, permanent offline image cache, external image embedding, automated unused-image GC, restore of a full production database backup and new load/capacity claims are NOT_RUN/not implemented. Per-document20 images/20MB includes retained historical and unreferenced uploads. Image bytes in PostgreSQL are a bounded first-version choice, not an object-storage scaling claim. Preserve prior failed load baseline and accepted local latency boundaries.
